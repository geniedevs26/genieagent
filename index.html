Global lead discovery coverage

The research engine searches six independent public search providers: Bing, DuckDuckGo, Google, Mojeek, Startpage and Ecosia. It rotates through search-result pages each hour, skips URLs that are not yet due for recheck in the persistent research history, and checks up to 300 source pages per run, with a cap of 50 qualified records per run.

Buyer sources

Official business websites and their project/contact pages; public request-for-proposal and request-for-quote pages; buyer-authored public community posts where replies are still open. Reddit buyer posts are checked as a separate source type.

Services matched

Websites, WordPress, Shopify, landing pages, web apps, Android apps, AI chatbots, workflow automation, Excel and data cleanup, dashboards, technical writing, content/SEO, graphic design, and Telugu-English translation.

Lead qualification

A record enters Leads only when the source shows a real buyer requirement, paid/contract intent, no unpaid or employment-only signals, and a usable public email, verified contact form, or public email address. Source evidence is retained.

Safety and owner control

Seller advertisements, generic job listings, unpaid requests, duplicates and unverifiable contact routes are excluded. Tailored prospect emails and bounded follow-ups run automatically to verified public email addresses; opt-outs and bounces stop contact. No calls are claimed. Your approval is required for pricing, payment, binding agreements and delivery commitments.

Important: the 300-page limit is a per-run inspection target, not a promise that 300 paid buyers exist. The system records only verified buyer requests; it never invents leads to fill a quota.

Permission and deal workflow

  1. Research & verify: the hourly agent searches global business websites and buyer communities, rotates through search-result pages and skips previously checked URLs until their scheduled recheck time. It separates paid-confirmed requests from budget-unconfirmed buyer prospects and retains source evidence.
  2. Initial outreach & follow-up: individually tailored emails are sent automatically to eligible prospects with verified public email addresses, with up to three follow-ups (days 5, 12 and 21) only when inbox monitoring finds no reply, bounce or opt-out.
  3. Client response: replies are monitored automatically. A pricing request receives an automatic scope/budget intake message; no quote or approval request is created yet. Only after the buyer explicitly confirms they want to proceed does a client-order opportunity and financial approval request appear.
  4. Proposal & agreement: you approve each exact document before the executor sends it. Financial approval, scope, price, costs, payment terms and evidence are mandatory before a proposal is sent.
  5. Project execution: project start, each milestone delivery, final delivery and order closure have separate approval requests. Nothing advances without your approval.
  6. Audit: sends, replies, bounces, approvals and stage transitions are recorded. No AI phone calls are claimed until a compliant calling provider is actually connected.

Integration status: Supabase and hourly public research are connected. SMTP/IMAP credentials are present in GitHub Actions. AUTO_OUTREACH_ENABLED=true: eligible first-contact emails and bounded follow-ups run automatically. Every genuine reply to an eligible outreach email receives automated scope/budget qualification; pricing, payment, agreements and other binding actions remain behind owner approval. Inbox monitoring is active and processes replies, bounces and opt-outs. Proposal, agreement, project-start, milestone, final-delivery and closure actions remain behind separate owner approvals. AI phone calling is not connected. Never store API keys in this page or public GitHub files.
