Permission and deal workflow

  1. Research: collect a candidate and source evidence. A search result is not confirmed demand.
  2. Verify: confirm the client requirement and public business contact. No outreach while the requirement is unconfirmed.
  3. Prepare: show the exact message or call script, recipient, scope, timeline, and costs.
  4. Your approval: ask separately for every email, call, proposal, and follow-up. Previous approval never authorizes later messages.
  5. Financial approval: block proposals until revenue, all costs, positive expected net profit, scope, budget, payment terms, and pricing evidence are checked.
  6. Log and continue: record provider results and ask again before the next external action.

Integration status: secure private storage and outbound email/calling providers are not connected yet. The app must not claim a message was sent or a call placed without provider confirmation. Never store API keys in this page or public GitHub files.
