Permission and deal workflow

  1. Research & verify: the hourly agent searches global business websites and buyer communities, rotates through unseen pages, and qualifies only explicit paid buyer requests with a verified public email, contact form, or active reply route.
  2. Initial outreach & follow-up: prospect emails are currently disabled in the scheduled workflow. When separately authorized and configured, the guarded sender can send individually tailored enquiries and up to three follow-ups (days 5, 12 and 21), only when inbox monitoring confirms no reply, bounce or opt-out.
  3. Client response: replies are monitored automatically. A request for pricing creates a client-order record and a pending financial-proposal approval.
  4. Proposal & agreement: you approve each exact document before the executor sends it. Financial approval, scope, price, costs, payment terms and evidence are mandatory before a proposal is sent.
  5. Project execution: project start, each milestone delivery, final delivery and order closure have separate approval requests. Nothing advances without your approval.
  6. Audit: sends, replies, bounces, approvals and stage transitions are recorded. No AI phone calls are claimed until a compliant calling provider is actually connected.
